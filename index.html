What is real, and what is imagined

One 3D world, one continuous camera path. z = log₁₀ of the field width in metres, from 1.3 nm to 10²⁷ m. Scroll, drag, use arrow keys, the slider, or the zoom buttons.

  • Real data: protein structures from the PDB, a human neuron reconstruction from NeuroMorpho, neuroimaging brain surfaces, OpenStreetMap and terrain tiles for Bangalore, NASA Earth and night-light imagery, JPL orbital elements, the HYG star catalogue, galaxy surveys and the Planck map of the early universe. Sources and licences are listed in each level's SOURCES file.
  • Invented: the woman, her flat and her building are illustrative. Her location is a neighbourhood-scale guess. The route to a data centre is illustrative.
  • Time is stretched: nerve signals take about a millisecond; here they take a few tenths of a second. Orbits are accelerated. Planets are drawn larger than true scale.
