# molecule level: sources and processing (fetched 2026-10-06)

| runtime file | source | licence | what was done |
|---|---|---|---|
| `receptor_lo.bin.gz`, `receptor_hi.bin.gz` | RCSB PDB **3KG2** (Sobolevsky, Rosconi, Gouaux 2009, Nature 462:745; full-length rat GluA2, 3.6 A) in the **OPM** membrane orientation (https://opm-assets.storage.googleapis.com/pdb/3kg2.pdb, Lomize et al. 2012); the four ligand-binding domains (S1S2, 3KG2 residues 393-506 / 630-773) are replaced by the glutamate-bound closed **1FTJ** LBD (Armstrong & Gouaux 1998; Neuron 20:... ; https://files.rcsb.org/download/1FTJ.pdb, 1.9 A) superposed on lobe D1 per chain | PDB: CC0; OPM: free for use with citation | atoms assembled by `levels/molecule/build/assemble.py`; Gaussian molecular surface (blobby, K=1.7, iso=1.5) by marching cubes (`surface.py`, `surface2.py`), exactly clipped at the section plane z=0 and capped with a constrained Delaunay triangulation; per-vertex colour (chain/domain), ambient occlusion from blurred occupancy, lobe-D2 and TM weights; a 0.22 nm pore lumen is carved through the closed channel so it can dilate |
| `pocket.json` | 1FTJ chain A: glutamate (resname GLU, HETATM 274), residues within 0.42 nm, crystallographic waters within 0.55 nm; contacts re-derived from coordinates | CC0 | hydrogens on glutamate added with RDKit (L-Glu zwitterion, heavy atoms constrained); H-bond list = ligand N/O...protein N/O <= 0.335 nm; hinge axis/pivot/angle of D2 relative to D1 fitted from the 3KG2 (ZK200775-open) vs 1FTJ (glutamate-closed) LBDs (24.7-25.5 deg); Verified contacts: Pro478 O, Thr480 (OG1, N), Arg485 (NH1, NH2), Ser654 (N, OG), Thr655 (OG1, N), Glu705 (OE1/OE2) and Tyr450 packing, matching the literature numbering Y450/P478/T480/R485/S654/T655/E705 |
| `lipid_popc.json`, `lipid_place.bin.gz` | POPC topology/stereochemistry (RCSB chemical component POV, `POV_ideal.sdf`) | CC0 | 3-D conformers generated with RDKit (ETKDG + systematic torsion sampling of the glycerol/head torsions, all-trans tails with sampled gauche defects, cis-9 kink in the oleoyl chain, steric filter at 0.30 nm, tails parallel within ~22 deg); 8 diverse conformers instanced on a hexagonal lattice (0.65 nm^2 per lipid, +-0.1 nm jitter, random azimuth); phosphate planes at +-1.95 nm from the bilayer centre (hydrophobic thickness 3.18 nm from OPM, headgroup-to-headgroup ~3.9 nm); lipids overlapping protein atoms (< 0.30 nm) removed. NOTE: this is not a molecular-dynamics-equilibrated bilayer (no public POPC coordinate set could be downloaded); chain conformations are sampled, not simulated |
| `water.bin.gz` | GROMACS `spc216.gro` (equilibrated 216-molecule SPC cell, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gromacs/gromacs/main/share/top/spc216.gro) | LGPL-2.1 data file | tiled periodically; molecule positions and orientations kept, drawn with TIP3P geometry (O-H 0.0957 nm, HOH 104.52 deg); density 33.4 / nm^3; removed where overlapping the protein or the bilayer core; Na+/Cl- at ~150 mM chosen at random from water sites |

Raw downloads and intermediates are in `raw/`; the pipeline scripts are in `levels/molecule/build/` (`assemble.py` -> `surface2.py hi|lo`, `pocket.py`, `lipid2.py`, `place.py`, `water.py`).

Known gaps: no glycans, no detergent; the 1FTJ LBDs are placed in all four subunits (3KG2 itself carries the antagonist ZK200775, so a glutamate-bound closed LBD in every pocket is a model, not a crystal structure of the full-length receptor with glutamate); the TM domain is the closed-gate 3KG2 conformation, the dilation of the pore on a keystroke is a radial displacement of the pore-lining helices (illustrative); lipid chains are torsion-sampled, not MD-equilibrated.
