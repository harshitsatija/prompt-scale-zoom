## What in `web_pts.bin` is REAL and what is SYNTHETIC
* kind 0 = real 2MRS galaxy, kind 1 = real SDSS galaxy (positions real; "fingers of god" collapsed: friends-of-friends groups in (1.3-1.6 Mpc transverse, 15-18 Mpc line-of-sight) have their line-of-sight scatter squeezed to 12-15 %; this is a display correction of redshift-space distortion, not a measurement).
* kind 3 = "companions": ~500 k extra points (296 k around SDSS galaxies beyond ~200 Mpc to offset flux-limited thinning; ~200 k faint companions around 2MRS galaxies within ~300 Mpc, sigma 0.5-1.5 Mpc) scattered (Gaussian) around real galaxies to compensate for the flux-limited thinning of the surveys. Synthetic, tied to real structure.
* kind 2 = synthetic fill, a Zel'dovich-approximation LCDM realisation (256^3 particles in a 768 Mpc periodic box, BBKS spectrum, sigma8 = 0.81, smoothed at 5 Mpc, biased tracer weighting), tiled with random rotations/reflections/shifts to fill the observable universe (radius 14,260 Mpc). It is used (a) outside the SDSS footprint (footprint mask derived from the SDSS sample itself, 33.8 % of sky) - except inside the 2MRS volume (r < 250 Mpc, |b| > 8 deg), where only real 2MRS galaxies and their companions are drawn (mock fills only the Galactic zone of avoidance there) - (b) beyond the SDSS depth (> 2300 Mpc, thinned with radius so the point count stays displayable). Its radial density follows the measured SDSS sample density n(r), so the inside/outside of the footprint look statistically alike. It is NOT a map of real structure; far from Earth no real survey exists at this resolution.
* The Milky Way is at the origin; "Laniakea" and the "Sloan Great Wall" labels sit on literature positions and no outline is drawn (the true Laniakea boundary needs the Cosmicflows-4 velocity field, which was not fetched).

Note: the raw downloads (2mrs.csv, sdss.csv, smica1024.fits, 240 MB) were moved out of the repo to keep the level under the size budget; re-fetch with the URLs above into assets/web/raw/ to re-run levels/web/build_data.py -> pack.py -> build_cmb.py. Runtime size: web_pts.bin 20.9 MB + cmb_smica.webp 2.5 MB.

### Display-only things that are NOT data (all documented so nothing is passed off as measurement)
* Laniakea dotted outline: a schematic sphere, 160 Mpc across (Tully+2014 quote ~520 Mly), centred on `lan` (Great-Attractor basin). The real boundary needs the Cosmicflows-4 velocity field (not fetched).
* Bootes void outline: a sphere of 50 Mpc radius (330 Mly across, Kirshner+1981) on its literature position.
* The whole web is rotated about the Milky Way (blend over z 24.7-25.3, `ROT` in `levels/web.js`) so the labelled structures land inside the frame; the survey's own axes put Shapley/Sloan Great Wall/Bootes off the top of the frame at the zooms where they are labelled. Galaxy-level continuity (z < 24.7) uses the unrotated frame.
* Slab rendering: for z < 26.7 only galaxies within +-1.1 field widths of the focus plane are drawn (a thick slice, like a survey wedge plot), so structure is not buried under 2.6 M line-of-sight points. Beyond 26.7 the whole volume is drawn.
* Deep fill homogenisation: the synthetic Zel'dovich mock beyond ~2300 Mpc was thinned (~r^-3) to keep the point count displayable. For the pull-back to the edge (morphing in over z 26.1-26.8) each mock point is re-drawn at a radius uniform in comoving volume (same direction), because the real universe is homogeneous there and a thinned fill read as a bright core with a ring. At that zoom one pixel is > 100 Mpc so no web structure is lost.
* Brightness is density-weighted (void points dim, cluster points bright) so filaments and voids read; real 2MRS/SDSS galaxies are drawn 2.4x brighter than synthetic fill/companions.
* The backdrop's flare stars are replaced by plain violet from z=23.4 (a screen quad that reproduces the engine's violet well without the stars): nothing exists outside the observable universe.
* Known data gap: fingers-of-god in SDSS beyond z~0.14 (600 Mpc) are not collapsed (the display correction in build_data.py stops there); at z~25.7 a few radial streaks remain.
