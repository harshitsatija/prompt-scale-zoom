# synapse level: sources, licences, processing

All runtime files here are produced by the scripts in `v4/levels/synapse/` (`build_proteins.py`, `build_core.py`, `build_org.py`, `build_far.py`, `build_layout.py`, `build_mass.py`; helpers `pdbio.py`, `sdf.py`, `shapes.py`, `common.py`). Re-run them with `python3.11` (numpy, scipy, scikit-image, trimesh, fast-simplification). Raw downloads are in `src/` (gzip-compressed mmCIF / PDB, fetched 2026-10-06; RCSB PDB data are CC0 / public domain).

## Real molecular structures (RCSB PDB, AlphaFold DB) -> `proteins.bin/.json`
Each entry is converted to a Gaussian molecular surface (histogram of all heavy atoms on a 1.1-4 A grid, Gaussian filter sigma 2.5-9 A, isosurface chosen so the enclosed volume equals 17 A^3 per heavy atom, marching cubes, quadric decimation, normals from the mesh, baked ambient-occlusion tint from a 24 A density blur), oriented with the membrane normal on +y, vertex colour = domain/subunit of the nearest 3 atoms. Coordinates are in nm (1 A = 0.1 nm), true scale. Detail levels (extra-fine / fine / medium / coarse) are separate meshes of the same atoms, chosen per instance by distance from the focus.

## Procedural but constrained (SDF + marching cubes), `cells_mass.*`, `cells_*`
- `psd_mass`: the postsynaptic density as a union of ~1700 small bumpy protein clusters (3-5 lobes of 1.3-2.5 nm) 10-41 nm below the inner leaflet, thin linkers between neighbours, and 30 receptor C-terminal tails wandering from each receptor foot into the scaffold; the real-structure PSD-95 / CaMKII instances sit on top of it. Depth layering after immuno-EM (Valtschanoff & Weinberg 2001 J Neurosci 21:1211; Dani et al. 2010 Neuron 68:786). `psd_env` is the same object as a smooth, ragged slab 25-45 nm thick used when a protein is below ~2 px (z > -6.2).
- `az_mass`: the active-zone dense projections as 21 conical clusters (16-30 nm tall) of bumpy 3-4 lobe proteins on a ~55 nm hex lattice, Bassoon/Piccolo-like filaments rising from them, none inside docked vesicles; `az_env` its low-zoom envelope. (Fernandez-Busnadiego et al. 2010 J Cell Biol 188:145; Maschi & Klyachko 2017 Neuron 94:65.)
- Cells (`cells_pre/post/org/far/farpre/bg`): signed-distance models meshed with marching cubes (14-55 nm voxels), dimensions after serial-section EM morphometry (not traced from a volume): bouton 0.9 x 0.6 x 0.74 um with ~280 vesicles (140 behind the section plane), spine head 0.76 um, neck 0.13 um narrowing to 0.13 um over 1.2 um, shaft radius 0.7 um, cleft 23 nm, PSD ~0.3 um x 40 nm, AZ 0.4 um (Harris & Stevens 1989 J Neurosci 9:2982; Schikorski & Stevens 1997 J Neurosci 17:5858; Bourne & Harris 2008 Annu Rev Neurosci 31:47; Lucic et al. 2005 PNAS 102:9631). The astrocytic process is a fenestrated lamella 32-90 nm thick, 20 nm from the neurites, with 110 glycogen granules (Witcher et al. 2007 Glia 55:13; Ventura & Harris 1999 J Neurosci 19:6897).
- Membranes: the section plane cuts every bilayer; the cut is drawn as a 4.8 nm POPC-like cartoon (head groups 0.66 nm apart, two tails per lipid, glycerol dot) whenever a lipid is more than ~6 px wide, and as a two-tone line otherwise. Windows are cut in the lines where the plane passes through a membrane protein (AMPAR, NMDAR, Cav) so its transmembrane cut face shows. The surface of the membrane is a hexagonal head-group shader.
- Actin: 70 filaments with Arp2/3 branches at 70 deg (Hotulainen & Hoogenraad 2010 J Cell Biol 189:619), drawn with the 37 nm crossover; 3 + 7 microtubules (25 nm, 13 protofilaments); mitochondrion with 14 nm cristae lamellae; 3 fenestrated cisternae (spine apparatus); 70 free ribosomes + polyribosome; 12 endosome-like vesicles.

## Gaps (honest)
- Cell shapes are modelled from morphometry, not traced from an EM volume (no public volume could be re-meshed to the contract geometry).
- Proteins are Gaussian surfaces at 2.5-9 A: no side chains or backbone; PSD-95 is an AlphaFold prediction (compact; the real molecule is flexible); the PSD filler and the dense projections are not individual molecules.
- Vesicle contents, SNARE zippering, receptor gating and the depolarisation wave are illustrative.
- At z < -7.55 the synapse stops drawing the hero receptor (the molecule level draws it, from the same PDB entry); the two levels each draw their own lipids for z in [-7.55, -7.3].

## Round-2 changes (after blind review)
- `layout.json`: +70 reserve/recycling-pool synaptic vesicles (total 210 in the z<=0 half) and +65 synapsin tethers appended by `levels/synapse/add_ves.py` (free-space sampling in the bouton SDF, 45.5 nm centre spacing; Schikorski & Stevens 1997, Rizzoli & Betz 2005, Wilhelm 2014). Existing indices untouched.
- `cells_org.bin`: rebuilt by `build_org.py`: lamellar cristae (alternating infoldings with narrow crista junctions, bulbous rims, undulation; Nicastro 2000, Mannella 2006) and a spine apparatus of 4 rounded-rim cisternae (22 nm lumen, 51 nm pitch) joined by dense bridges (Spacek & Harris 1997).
- Procedural (no external data): lipid headgroup spheres (0.72 nm hex lattice) on the postsynaptic outer leaflet, cut-through-protein plugs in the section ribbons, cleft backdrop gradient, far-mitochondria cristae lamellae.

## Round-3 changes (after blind review)
- New real data: `src/1FTJ.cif.gz` (RCSB, CC0; GluA2 S1S2 ligand-binding core with L-glutamate, Armstrong & Gouaux 2000 Neuron 28:165, fetched 2026-10-06). `levels/synapse/build_glu.py` superposes the 1FTJ LBD onto each of the four 3KG2 LBDs (Kabsch on 249 sequence-aligned C-alpha, rmsd ~2.6 A because 3KG2 is the open antagonist-bound state) and writes the 10 glutamate heavy atoms per clamshell to `glu_pose.json` (nm, receptor frame). Drawn as ball-and-stick on the hero receptor (hidden when the molecule level draws it).
- `cells_mass`: PSD rebuilt as a biconvex lens (30-45 nm thick, ragged 340 nm wide, tapering to a rounded rim; ~2300 clusters + a ~8 nm electron-dense lamina of darkest clusters directly under the membrane), `psd_env` is the same lens; PSD-95 copies 260 -> 330. `psd_layout.json` regenerated.
- `cells_org`: presynaptic ER rebuilt as a branching tubular network mostly behind the section plane (plus a small cisterna) instead of a ribbon in the plane; dendritic ER rerouted as a tubular network that never crosses the shaft mitochondria; spine apparatus cisternae now tilted, ragged-rimmed and fenestrated (round holes), with dense bridges; astrocytic process gains thin irregular leaflets (fingers 25-60 nm) and 220 glycogen granules; cleft mouth kept clear of astrocyte.
- `layout.json`: 59 vesicles re-seated by `fix_ves.py` (clear of the new ER, the mitochondrion and the axon neck) and the synapsin tether list recomputed (128 pairs).
- Procedural (no external data): cleft depth haze (`haze.js`: five blurred billboards of AMPAR/NMDAR-, neurexin-neuroligin-, N-cadherin- and Cav-like silhouettes at true size beyond z = -0.2 um), ~190 extra perisynaptic copies of the real neurexin-neuroligin / N-cadherin / AMPAR / NMDAR meshes out to 0.55 um, per-lipid jitter of the headgroup spheres, cholesterol rods and leaflet asymmetry in the bilayer cartoon, transmembrane helix bundles (6+5 tilted helices, 5.4 A pitch banding) replacing the box plugs where the section plane cuts a receptor, microtubule/organelle cross-section texture and surface variation on context cells, interior shading of cut protein caps.
- Axonal microtubules now stop in the axon neck (capped) and do not run across the bouton mitochondrion. Membrane section lines recede at tissue scale (no outlines); vesicle rings keep theirs.
