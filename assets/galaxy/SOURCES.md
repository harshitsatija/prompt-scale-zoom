# assets/galaxy: sources and what was done to them (fetched 2026-10-06)

Rebuild everything with `cd v4/levels/galaxy && python3 gen_lg.py && python3 gen_mw.py && python3 gen_others.py` (needs numpy, scipy, Pillow).

## Real data (positions are real)
| file | source | licence | processing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mcc.tsv` (raw), `localgroup.json` | McConnachie 2012, AJ 144, 4, "The Observed Properties of Dwarf Galaxies in and around the Local Group", VizieR `J/AJ/144/4/catalog` (https://vizier.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/asu-tsv?-source=J/AJ/144/4/catalog). The brief's CADC page was unreachable from here; VizieR hosts the same catalogue (101 galaxies with distances). | CDS/VizieR open data, cite the paper | RA/Dec/distance -> galactic -> scene frame (x=-Y_gal, y=Z_gal, z=-X_gal, kpc, Sun at origin). For M31, M33, LMC, SMC the inclination and position angle (M31 77 deg / 37.7; M33 56 / 22.5; LMC 34.7 / 122.5) give the disc basis `ex,ey` (near side = PA-90). |
| `harris_gc.tsv` (raw), `gc.json` | Harris (1996, 2003 ed.) globular cluster catalogue, VizieR `VII/202/catalog` (145 clusters with l, b, d, Vt) | CDS/VizieR open data | l,b,d -> scene frame, kpc |
| spiral-arm numbers in `levels/galaxy/gen_common.py` and `model.js` | Reid et al. 2019, ApJ 885, 131, Table 2 (arXiv:1910.03357, the PDF/text is in `v4/refs/galaxy/reid.pdf|txt`): kink azimuth, kink radius, pitch angles, widths, R0 = 8.15 kpc. | arXiv/ApJ, used as numerical parameters | Norma and Outer are treated as one arm (outward pitch 9.3 deg, which reproduces R=12.2 kpc one turn later); the Sgr-Car inward pitch was set to 4 deg (Reid: 1.0 +- 2.1, ill constrained) so the arm keeps winding into the bar end; arms are extended beyond the parallax range to R=15.5 kpc. |

## Reference imagery (viewed for comparison, NOT shipped in the page)
`v4/refs/galaxy/`: `hurt.jpg` (NASA/JPL-Caltech/ESO/R. Hurt, public domain), `m31_ha.jpg` (Adam Evans, CC BY 2.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Andromeda_Galaxy_(with_h-alpha).jpg), `m33.jpg` (Francesco Antonucci, CC BY-SA 4.0), `reid.pdf`.

## Round 2 changes (2026-10-06)
- gen_mw.py: bar narrowed/brightened (half-length 4.3 kpc, 27 deg), interarm disc dimmed, arms and dust lanes made crisp (dust on the inner edge, Reid+2019 pitch unchanged), HII knots stronger; maps now quality 97 (glow) / lossless (dust) to remove webp block artefacts; two new star sets `bright`/`loc_bright` (7,800 OB-type stars, luminosity-function tail) drawn larger with a soft halo. Procedural, not Gaia.
- gen_others.py: gx maps re-saved at quality 97 / lossless dust.
- galaxy.js: far-field galaxies (MW, M31, M33, LMC, SMC, M32, NGC 205) are drawn through a per-galaxy exaggeration k = max(1, minPx / physicalPx) about their REAL catalogue centres (positions exact, sizes exaggerated only where otherwise sub-pixel; shrinks to near-physical by z=24). LMC/SMC/M32/NGC 205 are camera-facing blobs (irregular / Sersic n=2.5 and 1.6 profiles drawn procedurally); M31 and M33 are inclined textured discs plus a soft halo so they never collapse to a needle. M32 / NGC 205 positions from McConnachie 2012.
- Local Group shell: a faint rim-lit sphere (radius 1.15 Mpc, centred 55% of the way from the MW to M31) = schematic extent of the group, not a measured surface.
- Magellanic Stream: 2,600 procedural points on a Bezier arc from the SMC towards the south galactic pole; schematic only (no HI data used).
- Still NOT real: per-star positions in the MW (statistical), real M31/M33 imagery (CC-BY/NASA images were not mapped), handedness of M31/M33 spirals.

## Round 3 changes
- gen_common.py/gen_mw.py: arms re-anchored at the bar tips (Perseus and Scutum-Centaurus start at the two bar ends, R=4.1 kpc, solved log-spiral inner pitch 14.8 deg for Perseus), minor arms fade in/out, along-arm clumps and gaps, 4 schematic spurs, brighter smooth exponential disc, wider boxy bar, yellower bulge, stronger dust on the inner edge. Still procedural (Reid 2019 geometry).
- galaxy.js: local-map blend made radial and detail-noise octaves rotated (no square seams); Sgr dwarf label 85,000 ly (catalogue 26 kpc); Magellanic Stream made very faint and labelled schematic; LMC/SMC gain lowered so the bar/arm reads; M31 enlarged (about 2.2x the MW) and brightened, M33 reduced; Local Group shell is now a soft ball with no rim; label placement fixed.
- New: neighbouring groups and clusters (M81, Sculptor, Cen A/M83, IC 342, M94, M101, Leo I, Virgo 16.5 Mpc, Fornax 19.3 Mpc): real RA/Dec/distance of the group centres (standard values from memory of the literature, not fetched this round); individual member positions are statistical.

## Round 4 changes
- NEW REAL DATA: `ungc_raw.tsv` / `ungc.json` (gen_ungc.py): Karachentsev+ 2013 Updated Nearby Galaxy Catalog, VizieR J/AJ/145/101/catalog (869 galaxies to 26 Mpc; 761 kept after dropping Local Group members already in McConnachie 2012). Real RA/Dec/distance, linear diameter, B-band absolute magnitude, T-type, axis ratio. Drawn as procedural elliptical/spiral/irregular glyphs (position angles random; spiral pattern schematic). Replaces the statistical M81/Cen A/Sculptor/IC342/M94/M101/Leo I scatter; only Virgo (700) and Fornax (300) members remain statistical (centres real).
- McConnachie dwarfs now drawn as type glyphs (dE/dSph smooth warm, dIrr blue irregular) visible at the Local Group zoom.
- MW maps regenerated: shorter major arms, stronger Orion Spur, brighter smooth disc between arms, blurred soft dust lanes. Local Group shell: rim-lit ball, no dark hole. Magellanic Stream: diffuse ribbon (still schematic path, no HI data).
- Perf: MW stars/planes/Stream hidden by z when not visible.
- Not done: real NASA imagery on M31/M33 (still procedural), the backdrop grid artefact (core; see v4/requests/galaxy.md).

## Round 5 changes
- shaders.js: the galaxy-glyph shader now soft-caps its output (own cap 0.62). The engine bloom kernel is under-sampled (taps 2.2 texels apart), so any pixel above ~0.82 luminance printed a dotted plaid halo around dwarf galaxies ("square halos"); keeping glyph peaks below the bloom threshold removes it. (Remaining backdrop g3 grid: core, see requests/galaxy.md.)
- gen_mw.py: arms broadened (wide band 4.6 w + 0.45 kpc), brighter and longer-scale smooth disc (R_d 4.2 kpc display profile) so the interarm region reads as stars rather than black gaps; maps and 504k stars regenerated.
- galaxy.js: near-field Milky Way (disc planes, stars, Sun) sits in `mwRoot`, scaled up to 1.55x about the galactic centre between z 21.55 and 22.5 (exaggeration, labelled MW label tracks it) so the galaxy is not a 150 px blob at z 21.8; far-field MW disc min size raised.
- Magellanic Stream: now ~5x fainter, narrower, knotted (HI is a chain of clumps), plus a Magellanic Bridge between SMC and LMC; still schematic (no HI data).
- Local Group shell: radius 1.2 Mpc (zero-velocity surface, McConnachie 2012), only fades in at z 23.2-23.7 when the whole sphere is in frame; label anchored on the barycentre.
- Verified from localgroup.json: M31-M33 = 206 kpc (0.67 Mly), M31-M32 = 23 kpc, M31-NGC 205 = 42 kpc, MW-M31 = 787 kpc: positions are catalogue values; the apparent M33 proximity is projection plus the small-pixel exaggeration of the discs.
- Labels: LMC/SMC placed on opposite sides; neighbour-group labels reduced to M81 and Cen A (plus Virgo/Fornax) so the Local Group is the one highlighted label at z 24.
