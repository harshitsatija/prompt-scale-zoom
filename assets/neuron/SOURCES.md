### What was chosen and why
Of the 51 Allen human neurons with axon, 102565 was selected by a search (`levels/neuron/build_assets.py`) for the one that can be rigidly rotated so that (a) its apical axis points along the cortical outward normal within 7.6 deg and (b) a real basal dendrite node with Rall radius ~0.7 um lies 35.4 um from the soma centre at the polar angle (68 deg) that the synapse level's shaft position demands, with an almost-horizontal tangent (|t.x| = 0.94). The whole morphology is scaled by 0.96 so the node lands exactly on S - (0, 2.36 um). Over +-12 um the dendrite is then bent straight along +x (radius 0.7 um) so that it coincides with the synapse level's shaft; this is the only edit to the real geometry.

## Everything else is procedural (no external data)
* soma membrane radial field, nucleus/nucleolus/chromatin, rough ER cisternae, Golgi stack, mitochondria, lysosomes, lipofuscin, vesicles: parametric, laid out after the textbook organisation (Alberts, Molecular Biology of the Cell; Peters/Palay/Webster "Fine structure of the nervous system"; reference images in `refs/neuron/`).
* spines: ~9,500 instanced spines (thin 55 %, mushroom 27 %, stubby 18 %; proximal 20 um spine-free; density 0.85/um basal, 0.58/um apical) in 3 LODs; measured spine shapes from Harris & Kater 1994 / Bourne & Harris 2008 (head 0.15-0.45 um, neck 0.1-0.15 um, length 0.5-1.5 um).
* myelin: 1.7 um nodal gaps, 95-150 um internodes starting ~65 um from the soma, sheath thickness 0.5 um with paranodal bulbs (CNS myelin, oligodendrocyte). Human internodes are longer than shown in places; the lengths are shortened so that several nodes are visible in one frame.
* neuropil: 620 random-walk neurites kept clear of the hero's own branches, capillary 7 um lumen with biconcave RBCs (Evans-Fung profile, 7.4 um), astrocyte, microglia, two neighbouring neurons drawn from coarse copies of the same real morphology rotated about the cortical normal.

## Round-2 changes (procedural, no new external data)
Tube sweeps now close their start as well as their end (no open or sawn-off ends); soma-root dendrites and the axon get explicit flares (axon hillock cone 2.3 um -> 0.5 um AIS over ~9 um); myelin starts ~38 um from the soma, nodes 3 um; neuropil tubes are kept behind the section plane (reflected, tapered, rounded) instead of being clipped; varicosities are oriented along their fibre; microglia recoloured. The hard capsule cut on the hero dendrite was replaced by a whole tube; the soma scoop is a soft slope.

## Round-3 changes (procedural, no new external data)
* Spines: density raised to 1.5 / um (basal) and 1.15 / um (apical) from 10-30 um path length (human L2/3 pyramidal cells are spinier than rodent: ~1-2 / um; Elston & Rosa, Benavides-Piccione 2013 range), mushroom / thin / stubby mix 30 / 50 / 20 %, narrower stubby base and neck flare; spine LOD0 tessellation 26 x 18. The hero spine footprint is kept clear.
* Hero synapse: bouton rebuilt as a lathe with a flat active-zone contact face (0.7 um, wraps the 0.74 um spine head), vesicle cloud (40 nm, non-overlapping, 20 x 14 spheres, denser at the active zone, hidden when cut by the section plane instead of showing as cups), single rounded PSD plate (~35 nm) on a flattened head top, thin presynaptic grid, axon neck with varicosities. 12 neighbouring boutons are placed on spines within 12 um of the hero spine; their axon strands are smooth splines.
* Soma: the local soma basis was left-handed, which made the organelle/nucleus group rotate wrongly (labels and ER sheets were misplaced); now right-handed. Soma ellipsoid 9.0 / 10.0 / 8.0 um semi-axes, nucleus ~55 % of the soma, nucleolus r = 1.0 um; mitochondria toned to the warm palette. Dendrite and axon roots start inside the soma and are discarded inside the soma ellipsoid in the shader so the lumen is continuous with the cytoplasm.
* Tissue: neuropil processes meander (persistent curvature + jitter, Catmull-Rom resampled), darken with depth, big profiles thinned to 1-1.8 um; capillary wall more opaque; myelin sheaths carry a wrap texture. Varicosities and tree boutons are LOD-instanced (hi / lo tessellation, only near the focus).
* Labels: nucleolus / nucleus / spine / synapse / myelin / node positions fixed or extended in z.
